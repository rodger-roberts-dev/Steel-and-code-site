# Steel & Code — Programming Fundamentals I

## Pricing Strategy

This document defines the pricing structure for:

- PF1 Self-Paced
- PF1 Founding Cohort
- Future Live Cohorts
- Optional Add-Ons

Pricing may change over time without requiring changes to the core curriculum.

---

# Product 1 — PF1 Self-Paced

## Product

**Programming Fundamentals I: Python, Automation & Computational Modeling**

### Includes

- Full PF1 curriculum
- Approximately 32 instructional videos
- Steel & Code Workbench access
- Guided labs
- Independent challenges
- Knowledge checks
- Four module projects
- Computational modeling capstone
- Course updates during the access period
- Certificate of completion

---

# Self-Paced Pricing

## Launch Price

**$59**

This price is intended for the initial public launch while Steel & Code builds:

- Early customers
- Testimonials
- Course feedback
- Brand recognition
- Product validation

---

## Standard Price

**$79**

Once the course has been tested with real students and refined, the standard self-paced price may move to approximately:

**$79**

Possible future range:

**$79–$99**

The course should not be priced solely based on the number of videos.

**Instruction + Workbench + Labs + Projects + Computational Modeling**

---

# Self-Paced Access

Recommended initial access model:

**Lifetime access to PF1 Version 1.x**

Students receive access to the purchased PF1 course and improvements made to that version.

Future major courses or substantially different products are not automatically included.

This keeps the offer simple for early customers.

- Capstone feedback

# Founding Member Benefits

The first cohort helps test and refine Steel & Code.

Founding members may receive:

- $129 founding price
- Lifetime PF1 access
- Future PF1 course updates
- Founding member status
- Early access to selected Steel & Code features
- Opportunity to influence course improvements
- Continued access to the PF1 community

Founding benefits should remain simple and sustainable.

Do not promise unlimited future courses or unlimited instructor support.

# Discount Strategy

Steel & Code should avoid constant discounting.

Discounts may be used for specific situations such as:

- Founding cohort
- Launch promotion
- Student pricing
- Veterans
- Returning customers
- Bundles

The regular product price should remain credible.

Do not train customers to wait for sales.

---

# Refund Policy

A simple initial refund policy may be appropriate.

Possible starting policy:

**14-day refund period**

A student may request a refund within 14 days of purchase if they determine the course is not right for them.

For live cohorts, refund eligibility may end after a defined number of live sessions.

Final refund terms should be clearly displayed before purchase.

# Longer-Term Pricing Philosophy

Steel & Code should compete on the quality of the learning experience rather than being the cheapest Python course available.

The product combines:

- Practical instruction
- Coding environment
- Projects
- Automation
- Computational modeling
- Instructor experience
- Real-world applications

Pricing should reflect the complete system rather than the video count.

---

# Pricing Guardrails

Do not:

- Continuously discount
- Offer unlimited instructor access at low prices
- Include every future course for free
- Add expensive support without increasing price
- Compete directly with $10 marketplace courses on price

Instead:

- Keep the self-paced product accessible
- Charge more for instructor interaction
- Increase pricing as proof and demand increase
- Protect instructor time
- Improve the product before expanding the catalog
